### 🧭 About me

I'm interested in human–computer interaction in counseling, especially how AI agents talk with people seeking emotional support. When someone says, “Forget it. Talking won't change anything,” do you ask a follow-up question or acknowledge how they feel first? I want to understand how small choices like these affect whether someone feels comfortable saying more.

I'm also exploring how AI agents could play simulated clients so counselors can practice. When trainees review a session afterward, what cues would help them notice something they missed in the moment? That's a question I'd like to keep working on.
